Tips and Advice

Common Triggers

  • Stress and anxiety
  • Irregular sleep
  • Skipped meals
  • Bright or flickering lights

Relief Tips

  • Rest in a dark, quiet room
  • Stay hydrated
  • Apply a cold compress to your forehead

When to See a Doctor

Seek medical help if headaches are sudden and severe, come with fever, vision loss, or weakness, or keep getting worse.

Not medical advice. Please consult a doctor.
